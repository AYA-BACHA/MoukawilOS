What are the steps after registering as an auto-entrepreneur in Algeria?

Regulatory Assistant

After your registration is confirmed, you need to complete the following steps within the first 30 days:

  1. Obtain your tax identification number (NIF) from the tax inspection office (Centre des Impôts) in your registered wilaya. You will need your registration certificate and a copy of your national ID.
  2. Register with CASNOS (Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale des Non-Salariés) for social security coverage. This is mandatory even during the first year, though you benefit from a reduced rate.
  3. Open a dedicated bank account (CCP or bank account) for your business transactions. This is required for declaring revenue and receiving payments.
  4. Begin tracking your revenue from day one, as you will need to declare it quarterly via the G12bis form.

Your first quarterly declaration is due at the end of the quarter following your registration date.

Sources
Loi n° 22-18 du 25 juillet 2022 relative au statut de l'auto-entrepreneur — Art. 12-15

What happens if I exceed the 5 million DZD revenue threshold?

Regulatory Assistant

If your annual revenue exceeds 5 000 000 DZD, you lose the auto-entrepreneur status starting from the following fiscal year. Here is what that means in practice:

  1. You must register as a standard enterprise — either as a sole proprietorship (Entreprise Individuelle) or choose another legal form. This requires re-registration with the CNRC (Centre National du Registre de Commerce).
  2. Your tax regime changes from the IFU (Impôt Forfaitaire Unique) to the regime réel, which involves more detailed accounting and potentially higher tax obligations.
  3. You retain auto-entrepreneur status for the remainder of the year in which you crossed the threshold — the change takes effect on January 1 of the following year.

It is advisable to monitor your revenue closely as you approach 80% of the threshold (4 000 000 DZD) to prepare for the transition.

Sources
